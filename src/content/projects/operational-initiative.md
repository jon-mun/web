### Overview

As an operational initiative, I led the effort to build and self-host a comprehensive development system. The primary goal was to centralize all aspects of development operations, including knowledge management, team communication, issue tracking, and system observability.

### Highlights

- **DORA Metrics Tracking**: Integrated Apache DevLake to collect, track, and analyze overall development speed and operational performance using DORA metrics.
- **Self-Hosted Infrastructure**: Engineered a fully self-hosted internal stack comprising GitLab for version control, Mattermost for communication, and Outline for documentation.
- **Secure Networking**: Utilized Headscale and Tailscale to create a secure, private network mesh for the internal tools.

### The Results

- **Team**: 1 Lead
- **Impact**: Unified the development lifecycle into a single cohesive, trackable, and highly observable environment (monitored by Grafana and Loki).
