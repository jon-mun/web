### Overview

We built a rapid MVP using the T3 stack deployed on Vercel with Supabase as the data layer for Walpix, an Australian manufacturing company. Financial workflows were modeled as state machines with full audit trails. The system replaced every spreadsheet within 6 weeks, with real-time dashboards replacing monthly manual reports.

### The Challenge

A mid-size Australian manufacturer ran their entire financial operations — invoicing, purchase orders, inventory costing, and reconciliation — through a labyrinth of interconnected spreadsheets. One formula error could cascade across months of financial data.

### The Results

- **100%** Spreadsheets Eliminated
- **6-Week** Rapid MVP
- **E2E** Automation
